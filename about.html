Research focus

Connecting theory with collider tests

I'm a particle physicist specializing in dark matter phenomenology and BSM collider phenomenology. My work spans EFT-based dark sector models, LHC reinterpretation pipelines, and fixed-target experiments — always trying to connect theoretical frameworks with what can actually be tested at colliders.
