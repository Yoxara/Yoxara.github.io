ORCID 0000-0001-6792-1734  ·  INSPIRE-HEP 1785072  ·  Google Scholar
Abstract

I work on dark matter phenomenology and BSM collider physics — building EFT-based dark sector models and testing them against LHC data, recasting and reinterpretation pipelines, and fixed-target experiments. My path has taken me from Venezuela to Brazil and France and back to Brazil, always at the intersection of model-building and what colliders can actually tell us.
